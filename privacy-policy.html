MaterialMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

MaterialMate is an offline material-estimation app for home and DIY projects. The app does not require an account.

Information stored on your device

MaterialMate stores the following information only on your device:

  • Project names you create
  • Measurements, material assumptions, prices, estimates, and shopping-list items you enter
  • Local app settings and basic on-device usage counters

Android cloud backup is disabled for this information.

Information we do not collect

MaterialMate does not have a developer server and does not send your project names, measurements, prices, estimates, or shopping lists to us.

Advertising and consent

If advertising is enabled, Google AdMob may collect and process information such as the advertising identifier, device information, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data to serve, measure, and improve ads. Google may treat this data under its own privacy policies.

Where required, MaterialMate uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent and provide privacy choices. You can change available ad privacy choices in the app's Settings section when Google requires the privacy options entry point.

For details, review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob and data usage

Sharing

When you choose to share a shopping list or email an invoice, Android opens the sharing or email app you select. MaterialMate does not transmit that information itself or send email in the background.

Deleting information

You can remove saved estimates inside the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes locally stored projects and settings.

Children

MaterialMate is intended for adults and is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be posted at the privacy policy address listed in Google Play.

Contact

Use the developer contact email published on the MaterialMate Google Play listing for privacy questions.
